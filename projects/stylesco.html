Styles & Co.

Styles & Co. is a logo design project focused on creating a distinct visual identity for a fashion brand. The project explored typography, composition, and visual elements to develop a logo that feels recognizable, modern, and adaptable across different brand applications. Multiple design directions were explored to create a cohesive mark that represents the brand’s overall aesthetic.

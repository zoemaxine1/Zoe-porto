UX/UI DESIGN

Underground

A microsite designed from scratch to launch Underground, a new fashion app. The project explored the UX and UI process from initial concept and ideation through wireframing, prototyping, and final interface design in Figma, with designs created for both desktop and mobile. The design focuses on creating a bold yet intuitive experience that reflects underground fashion culture, with clear navigation and a clean visual system.

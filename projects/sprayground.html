Sprayground

An event microsite designed to support underfunded artists in the Bay Area. The project explored the process of developing a visual identity and digital experience from concept to final interface design, with a focus on creating a strong and cohesive online presence. The site highlights events, programs, and resources for artists, combining clear navigation with engaging visuals to encourage community participation and support.
